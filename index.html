THE DESIGNER

About Me

I’m Basel Khatib, a fashion designer currently studying Fashion Design in Italy at IUAD – Accademia della Moda in Naples.

My work explores elegance, femininity and contemporary tailoring, with a particular focus on corsetry, eveningwear and refined silhouettes.

From the first sketch to fabric selection and construction, I approach each design as a complete creative process — transforming an idea into a piece with its own identity.
